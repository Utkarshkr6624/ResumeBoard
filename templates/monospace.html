Monospace

For systems, infrastructure and data work, where a monospaced page is a claim about how you read. Consolas or its equivalent at 10.5pt on a 48 em measure, a hairline between every job and nothing else, and no colour to soften it. It costs characters per line: a sentence of prose needs a wider measure here than anywhere else in the set, and that is the trade the summary is making.

The numbers

Every figure below is a value in the template registry the builder ships (assets/js/render/templates.js), or a size derived from it by the same multipliers the document stylesheet uses. Body text is not the preset’s nominal point size: it is that figure corrected for the face’s optical size, which is why a 10 pt Helvetica preset renders at 11.73 pt here. Nothing on this page is a measured pass rate or a hiring outcome, because neither exists.

What it is for

Good at

  • Parsed applications: one column, no tables, no images, and a heading alone on its own line.
  • A long career that has to stay on one sheet. 9.98 pt is a deliberate density, not an accident.
  • About 96 characters a line, which is the wide end of what a page of prose can carry and fits a lot onto a sheet.
  • Black and white printing. The builder already treats this preset as print-safe, so every fill and hue is gone.
  • A document with a shape to it. The arrangement is named in the registry rather than bolted on in a stylesheet.
  • A page size of Letter, which is what most of the mail in that country is.

Bad at

  • A reader who wants the layout to say something about them. It is a document, not a signal.
  • Comfortable reading. 9.98 pt on a 96 character measure is the hardest thing on this page to read for long.
  • A naive text extractor. A monospaced face has no word-boundary cues, and extractors that guess words from spacing are measurably worse at it.
  • A house style of your own: this preset is the builder’s, not your organisation’s.

ATS profile

Clean

Structurally this is the plain case: one column, no tables, no text boxes, no images, and a section heading standing alone on its own line. Nothing on the published failure lists applies.

The arrangement does not change that. The lines and panels are borders and backgrounds on block elements, which is the same class of object as a rule.

It is print-safe as well as clean, which is a stronger claim than most presets can make: no fills, no hue, and hairlines thickened for toner.

What no layout can do is make a parse correct. The ATS guide is blunt about this, and no vendor publishes an accuracy figure, so this page will not invent one.

Still yours to get right

A clean layout with a placeholder employer named “Company 1” is still a document a parser discards. No template can fix a sentence that describes effort instead of effect. Start with the resume basics guide and the one on quantifying impact.
