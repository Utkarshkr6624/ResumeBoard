Minimal

No bullets, no rules, no colour. The only preset that drops the bullet glyph entirely, which means the spacing between one claim and the next has to do the work a bullet was doing. It is the quietest page here and the hardest to skim.

The numbers

Every figure below is a value in the template registry the builder ships (assets/js/render/templates.js), or a size derived from it by the same multipliers the document stylesheet uses. Body text is not the preset’s nominal point size: it is that figure corrected for the face’s optical size, which is why a 10 pt Helvetica preset renders at 11.73 pt here. Nothing on this page is a measured pass rate or a hiring outcome, because neither exists.

What it is for

Good at

  • A reader who scrolls properly. With no markers, a first pass in ten seconds finds very little.
  • Portfolios where the work is shown elsewhere and the document is a footnote to it.
  • Senior readers with a long history of reading exactly this page shape.
  • Long-form, unhurried applications: a mission statement, a funding proposal, anything that is read rather than scanned.

Bad at

  • Screening. Six seconds on a stack of resumes is the normal budget and this spends it.
  • Anything technical. There is no line for tools, no line for a stack, and no marker to attach either to.
  • A first impression. The page has no visual hook, which is a decision, not an oversight, and readers read it as one.
  • Print, where A4 is the default and a page without a rule is a page a photocopier may eat the edge of.

ATS profile

Clean

Structurally the simplest document in the set: one column, no tables, no images, no boxes, and section headings that stand alone on their line.

The missing bullet is not a parse risk. A text extractor reads the claims as consecutive lines under one heading, which is what they are.

It is also not a fix for a weak document. The ATS guide is blunt about this: no layout makes a vague line specific, and parsers read sentences the same way a person does.

Still yours to get right

A clean layout with a placeholder employer named “Company 1” is still a document a parser discards. No template can fix a sentence that describes effort instead of effect. Start with the resume basics guide and the one on quantifying impact.
