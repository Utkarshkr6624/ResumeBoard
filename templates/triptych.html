Triptych

The header cut into three ruled bands: the name, the headline under a rule, the contact run under another. Everything below the header is the plain single column, so the shape is doing one job at the top and nothing else.

The numbers

Every figure below is a value in the template registry the builder ships (assets/js/render/templates.js), or a size derived from it by the same multipliers the document stylesheet uses. Body text is not the preset’s nominal point size: it is that figure corrected for the face’s optical size, which is why a 10 pt Helvetica preset renders at 11.73 pt here. Nothing on this page is a measured pass rate or a hiring outcome, because neither exists.

What it is for

Good at

  • Documents that open with a long contact run. Email, phone, location and a profile link all get a line rather than a wrap.
  • A person with a strong one-line positioning statement worth giving its own rule.
  • One-column, single-paragraph-per-role documents where the entries are already short.
  • Print, where a ruled band is a structure a photocopier preserves.

Bad at

  • A short contact block. Three bands for two facts is a lot of furniture.
  • A name that wraps, or a long professional title, because the middle band is one line tall.
  • Printers that eat thin rules, since the bands are carried entirely by two hairlines.
  • Anyone who reads a band as a table of contents entry and expects a page number after it.

ATS profile

Clean

Still one column, still no tables, still no images. The three bands are paragraphs separated by borders, so a text extractor reads name, headline, contact, and then the sections in order.

That is the point of the shape: it is a visual arrangement that happens to be a linear one. The DOM order is the reading order and the rules are decoration.

If you are comparing it against classic and worried about parsers, this is the same document with a more decisive header.

Still yours to get right

A clean layout with a placeholder employer named “Company 1” is still a document a parser discards. No template can fix a sentence that describes effort instead of effect. Start with the resume basics guide and the one on quantifying impact.
