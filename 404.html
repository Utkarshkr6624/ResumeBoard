If you were following a share link

A share link carries the whole resume in the part of the address after the #, so it never touches a server. If the link opened a blank page, the resume still exists — open the builder and the app will read it from the address. If the link was truncated by a chat app or an email client, the payload is cut off and there is nothing to recover; export a file instead next time, since a file cannot be shortened without you noticing.

If you were looking for your saved resume and the app looks empty, your browser is not the one you saved in, or the site data was cleared. The export and storage notes explain exactly where a resume is kept and how to bring one back.
