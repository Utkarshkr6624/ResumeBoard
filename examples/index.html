What these examples are, and are not

Every person on these pages is fictional. The companies are fictional. The numbers are plausible rather than measured, which means they are useful for structure and tone and useless as claims. Do not carry a metric into your own resume because it read well here.

What transfers is the shape: what order the sections go in, how many entries fit on a page, which achievements are worth a line and which are padding, and how a specific job title is reframed for the role being applied for.

How "start from this" works

  1. Open any example and press Start from this. The whole resume is compressed into the link itself — there is no server involved in the handoff.
  2. The builder opens with that resume loaded, ready to edit. Your existing work is not silently overwritten: the loaded version goes through the same undo stack as any other change, so one keystroke of Ctrl + Z takes it back.
  3. Replace the placeholder contact details first, then work down the page. The live checks in the editor flag anything left over.

Prefer to work on your own? Open the builder and start from a blank resume, or press the sample button for a worked starting point.

Before you adapt any of them

Check the dates

These resumes were written in 2024. If you are applying now, the end dates are already in the past and every entry needs a refresh.

Delete, do not decorate

The strongest move is usually to keep only the two or three entries that are relevant and cut the rest. A shorter resume that matches beats a complete one that does not.

Write your own numbers

If you do not have a number, describe the scale instead: team size, budget, user count, deadline. "No metric available" is not a weakness; a fabricated one is.

Match the posting

Reorder the sections so the most relevant one is first for that specific application. The builder lets you reorder and hide sections per resume.
